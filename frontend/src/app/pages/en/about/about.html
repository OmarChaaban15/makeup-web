Behind the brush

Hi, I'm Yonaida.
I believe in beauty that doesn't disguise.

My path in makeup began with a deep fascination with light, textures and the way a carefully done look can transform a person's confidence.

After more than five years working in Ibiza, I have specialised in destination weddings, fashion editorials and audiovisual productions. On an island where the sunset light takes centre stage and the Mediterranean climate is demanding, my technique focuses on flawless, fresh and long-lasting skin.

I don't see makeup as a mask to hide behind, but as an art that reveals and enhances what makes you unrepeatable.
